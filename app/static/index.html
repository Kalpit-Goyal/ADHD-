How the pipeline is built

From raw EEG to next steps

Each recording is cleaned, split into 5-second epochs, and summarised with spectral and connectivity features. A subject-level score comes from averaging the model's per-epoch votes. Then the app hands you real-world next steps.

EEG Input
A 19-channel resting-state recording. Demo subjects are held out of training, so their scores are genuinely out-of-sample.

Spectral Features
Log band power in delta, theta, alpha, beta, and gamma, plus the theta/beta ratio and relative band power per channel.

Granger-causality estimates across every channel pair capture how activity flows between brain regions during each epoch.

Methodology

How the screening works

1
EEG Upload
A 19-channel recording is validated and resampled to 128 Hz.
2
Preprocessing
0.5–45 Hz band-pass, 50 Hz notch, per-epoch z-scoring, and artifact rejection.
3
Features
Five log band powers and θ/β per channel, relative power, and 171 directed edges.
4
SVM Vote
SelectKBest(150) → StandardScaler → RBF-SVM scores each 5-second epoch; the subject score is the mean.
5
Confirm
A positive screen points to an in-person clinical assessment — never a self-diagnosis.

Validation snapshot

Subject-wise, 3-repeat cross-validation

SelectKBest(150) → StandardScaler → RBF-SVM
69.4% Accuracy majority vote across 3 repeats
0.747 AUROC 95% CI 0.656–0.834
85.2% Sensitivity ADHD subjects detected
53.3% Specificity controls correctly cleared

Trained on 119 of 121 children; the two demo recordings are excluded so their predictions are out-of-sample. Metrics come from 3-repeat subject-wise CV; the served artifact is a single fit (single-model estimate 66.4% ± 1.3%). Single-site dataset, modest specificity, and a 0.5 threshold mean this model is a research prototype, not a clinical test.
